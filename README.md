🌍 Cohere
Where impact comes together.
🚀 Overview

Cohere is a verified impact platform that connects NGOs, Donors, Volunteers, and Organizations into a unified ecosystem. It ensures transparency, accountability, and real-time coordination of resources such as food, clothes, essentials, and funds.

Unlike traditional NGO platforms, Cohere focuses on verified impact, where every action—from donation to delivery—is tracked and validated.

💡 Problem Statement

Existing NGO systems suffer from:

Lack of transparency in fund/resource utilization
Poor coordination between donors, NGOs, and volunteers
No real-time tracking of impact
Low trust due to absence of verification
🎯 Solution

Cohere solves these challenges by providing:

A centralized platform for all stakeholders
A verified NGO system
Real-time resource tracking and coordination
Proof-based delivery validation
Impact visualization and reporting
